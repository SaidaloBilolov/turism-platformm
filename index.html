ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Connecting education with tourism careers

Tourism Workforce Platform is a concept for bringing learning, practical skills, skill profiles and career opportunities into one connected journey.

OUR MISSION

From learning to earning

Help tourism learners build relevant skills, practice professional scenarios, present their achievements and discover potential career paths.

OUR VISION

A connected tourism workforce

Support collaboration between students, universities and tourism employers through a clear digital career journey.

How it works

  1. Learn through structured content.
  2. Practice real-world tourism scenarios.
  3. Present your skills and progress.
  4. Connect with tourism organizations.
  5. Explore jobs and internships.

Who it serves

Students, universities, tourism companies and people interested in professional development in tourism.
